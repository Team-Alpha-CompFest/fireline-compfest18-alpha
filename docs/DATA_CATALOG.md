## Explicit scope classification

- **Core/production-oriented candidates:** raw NASA hotspot, BMKG weather, peat soil moisture, peatland spatial products, Kalimantan school/exposure export. These are candidates only; no production integration has been approved.
- **Supporting/validation:** 2010–2020 Kalimantan climate subset, Pontianak weather, station/province metadata, derived peatland-enriched hotspot tables.
- **Reference-only/excluded from production dashboard:** Portugal/Montesinho `forestfires.csv` and its analysis-ready output; national population reference unless the team approves its source/year.
- **Absent:** no citizen-report dataset or schema was physically found.
